#Quality control: Cookie weight analysis
## Project Objective
This project performs a statistical comparison of the weight of batches of cookies (vanilla vs. chocolate) to assess consistency and uniformity in production,
 identifying if there are significant deviations between both groups.
 ## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas for data manipulation)
- SciPy & NumPy (for statistical analysis and distributions)
- Matplotlib / Seaborn (for displaying histograms)
## Results
- The fundamental metrics (mean and standard deviation) were calculated for each cookie group.
- The histograms allow you to see weight dispersion and check if production meets established quality standards.
